The credit card section holds multiple masked text boxes and labels. Each masked textbox has a label indicating what the textbox is for. The credit/debit textbox is a 16 digit textbox to enter your credit card number. The CCV textbox is a 3 digit textbox to enter your CCV code. The Date textbox is a 4 digit textbox that requires numbers in the format of 'MM/YY' to be properly processed. If any of this information is not valid a seperate textbox will indicate so
