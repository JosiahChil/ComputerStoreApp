The 3, or 2 if you properly logged in, buttons are for as follows. The clear cart button will reset your card and bring you back to the customer form. The complete purchase button will finalize your purchase, inserting it into out database as well as opening a reciept in your broswer. The back to login form is specifically for users who entered through the Browse items button on the login form. This returns you to the login form so you can log in and properly order items. If this button is enabled your complete purchase button will be disabled
